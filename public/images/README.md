# SIDE B street-scene artwork

The images `side-b-nyc.png`, `side-b-la.png`, and `side-b-atl.png` were generated as original photographic-style artwork for SIDE B’s visual redesign.

They depict fictional environments inspired by early-1990s New York, Los Angeles, and Atlanta street/music-video culture. They are not archival photographs, photographs of the featured artists, or stills from the referenced music videos. The page’s moodboard identifies the images as AI-generated artwork.

Art direction: analog 35mm texture, graffiti, brick and stucco alleyways, metal shutters, pasted flyers, saturated paint, warm streetlight, and early-90s hip-hop/new jack swing visual energy. No real artist likenesses or readable logos were requested.

Original artwork was generated for this project; image binaries are bundled locally so backgrounds do not depend on remote photo services.

`side-b-graffiti-wall.webp` is original AI-generated decorative background artwork created for the early-90s graffiti refresh. It depicts a fictional straight-on brick-and-shutter wall with wildstyle aerosol marks, bubble outlines, paint drips, and peeled wheatpaste, in cobalt, lime, orange, and magenta. It is not an archival photograph or an existing artist's mural. It is bundled locally and used as a decorative page/hero/studio/closing background; paper panels and dark overlays preserve reading contrast.
